Questions people ask us

How do I list my property here?

Tap Post property, add the details and a few photos, and submit. Our team reviews every listing and usually publishes it within 24 hours.

Is posting a property free?

Yes. Posting your flat, shop or plot on this website is free.

Will buyers see my phone number?

Only if you want them to. By default, buyers chat with you on the website and request your number, and you decide who gets it. You can also choose to show your number to every logged-in buyer.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Pune. We work across the city, from Baner, Wakad and Hinjewadi to Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar, Kondhwa and NIBM, and our office is in Wanowrie. Owners can list property from anywhere in Maharashtra.

Can you arrange a site visit?

Yes. Tap Enquire or WhatsApp on any property and we will set up a visit at a time that suits you.

Still have a question?

Ask our team on WhatsApp, or drop by the office in Wanowrie. We reply within working hours.
